# Credits

Almost everything in Doodlefolk is drawn and synthesised by the app itself. The exceptions:

## Sounds

The campfire's crackle (`Sounds/fire-*`) is cut from two public-domain recordings, sliced into single crackles
and a roar bed by `tools/slice-fire.py` and replayed at random (see `Sound.Fire.cs`):

- "Fire Crackling" by **AntumDeluge**, CC0 1.0. https://opengameart.org/content/fire-crackling
- "Fireplace Sound loop" by **PagDev**, CC0 1.0. https://opengameart.org/content/fireplace-sound-loop

CC0 asks for nothing, but thank you both.
